# Sample Executive Alignment Report

> Synthetic demonstration only. No real customer, employee, or evidence data.
> Generated by the SITORA reference evaluator (`python -m evaluator --demo`).
>
> This is a generic illustration of the SITORA framework. OrcaTrain is a
> commercial application built on SITORA and is not part of this repository.

## 1. Alignment question

For the privileged-access workforce in 2026-Q1, are required Security
Awareness Training obligations completed, current, and evidenced in
authoritative systems?

## 2. Scope

- **Intent / obligation:** Security Awareness Training (policy-INFOSEC-0042)
- **Population:** privileged-access workforce
- **Period:** 2026-Q1
- **Evidence sources:** LMS (system of record), HRIS
- **Evaluation rules:** 2 deterministic checks (completion, currency)

## 3. Findings summary

| SITORA status | Count | Subjects |
|---|---:|---|
| Aligned | 1 | emp-001 |
| Drifting | 1 | emp-002 |
| Incomplete | 1 | emp-003 |
| Conflicting | 1 | emp-004 |
| Unverifiable | 1 | emp-005 |

**Overall population alignment:** 1 of 5 (20%) aligned; 4 of 5 (80%)
non-aligned, distributed across evidence-quality failure modes (drift,
missing evidence, conflicting records, unverifiable provenance).

## 4. Key findings

- **emp-001 — Aligned.** Attributable LMS record shows completion and
  currency within the period. No action required.
- **emp-002 — Drifting.** Attributable record shows completion, but the
  completion date (2025-05-03) precedes the period start. Training is
  expired and requires recertification.
- **emp-003 — Incomplete.** No evidence present for the obligation in any
  connected source. Owner must confirm assignment and evidence availability.
- **emp-004 — Conflicting.** LMS reports completion; HRIS compliance flag
  reports non-completion. Attributable sources disagree and require
  reconciliation before a conclusion can be drawn.
- **emp-005 — Unverifiable.** An evidence record exists but lacks
  attributable provenance (no source-system attribution). The condition
  cannot be evaluated until provenance is established. This is never
  treated as "Aligned."

## 5. What happens next (governed action)

Per the SITORA control loop, each non-aligned finding produces governed
actions. In the commercial OrcaEIS platform, these are routed, owned, and
tracked to closure. Illustratively:

| Finding | Governed action type | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| emp-002 (Drifting) | Remediation — assign recertification | L&D operations |
| emp-003 (Incomplete) | Review — confirm assignment and evidence source | HR operations |
| emp-004 (Conflicting) | Escalation — reconcile LMS vs HRIS | Data governance |
| emp-005 (Unverifiable) | Review — establish evidence provenance | System owner |

## 6. Limitations

This report evaluates alignment against the defined scope and evidence only.
It does **not** certify enterprise-wide regulatory compliance, provide legal
or regulatory advice, or guarantee that obligations outside this scope are
met. "Unverifiable" is never reported as "Aligned."
