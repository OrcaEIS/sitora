# SITORA Trademark Policy

This policy governs use of the trademarks **OrcaEIS**, **SITORA**, and
**OrcaTrain** (the "Marks"). The Marks are owned by OrcaEIS.

> An open-source license does not grant permission to use project or company
> marks. The Apache-2.0 license under which SITORA code is distributed grants
> only copyright and patent licenses — not trademark rights.

## 1. Permitted uses

You may use the Marks without prior written permission to:

- **Describe origin:** state that your software is "based on," "derived from,"
  or "compatible with" SITORA, provided the statement is accurate.
- **Fork and reference:** reference the SITORA project in documentation,
  academic work, or commentary.
- **Educational use:** teach, write, or present about SITORA.
- **Implement the spec:** build software that implements the SITORA
  specification and describe it as "SITORA-compatible," provided it passes the
  conformance suite when one is published.

## 2. Prohibited uses

You may **not** use the Marks to:

- Imply endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with OrcaEIS without written
  agreement.
- Claim to be "SITORA-certified," "OrcaEIS-certified," or "official" in any
  way. No third party may claim certification until a formal certification
  program exists (see §3).
- Use a Mark in a company name, product name, domain name, or logo that
  competes with OrcaEIS or suggests official status.
- Use the Marks in a way that could mislead users about compatibility or the
  open/commercial boundary.

## 3. Certification program

A formal partner and product certification program is planned for a later
phase. Until a published certification rubric, assessment, versioning policy,
quality process, and renewal mechanism exist, **no third party may claim to be
"SITORA-certified."** Until then, partners are referred to as "implementation
collaborator," "design partner," "early delivery partner," or "pilot
implementation partner."

## 4. Logos and branding

Official logos are provided separately and may be used only under a brand
assets license. Do not alter, recolor, or combine the official logos without
permission. Forks and derivative works must not use the official OrcaEIS logo
in a way that implies they are official OrcaEIS distributions.

## 5. Reporting misuse

Report suspected trademark misuse to trademarks@orcaeis.com.

## 6. Changes

OrcaEIS may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in GitHub
Discussions and recorded in the repository history.

## 7. No waiver

Failure to enforce this policy in one instance does not waive OrcaEIS's right
to enforce it in any other instance.
